Research

Teaching Bangla question answering to say “I’m not sure”.

A model that always answers confidently is hard to trust. My FYDP looks at systems that measure their own uncertainty and act on it.

Illustration of the idea, not experimental results. Hover or focus a phrase to see its confidence.

1
RetrieveFind passages related to the question
2
GenerateDraft an answer from those passages
3
CalibrateEstimate how sure the model really is
4
DecideAnswer, retrieve again, or flag a possible hallucination
